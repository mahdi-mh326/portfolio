A real-time driver safety system designed to detect early signs of driver fatigue and micro-sleeps to prevent road accidents.

  • Developed using Python, OpenCV, and dlib facial landmark predictor.
  • Monitors facial landmarks and calculates Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) continuously.
  • Triggers immediate auditory buzzer/alarm when EAR drops below threshold.
