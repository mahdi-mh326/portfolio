Ongoing Investigation

Personalized Medical Recommendation System Using Explainable AI

Developing an advanced machine learning framework incorporating Explainable AI (XAI) algorithms—specifically SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations)—to deliver transparent, personalized medical recommendations.

The project bridges the critical interpretability gap between complex deep ensemble models and clinical validation, providing physicians with human-understandable visual feature attributions to support diagnostic decision-making.

Ongoing Investigation

Emotion Detection from Facial Images

Designing and evaluating deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architectures to detect and classify subtle human emotional states from facial images using computer vision preprocessing techniques.

The research benchmarks multiple architectures against challenging variations in facial orientation, ambient illumination, occlusion, and demographic diversity to attain robust real-time inference.
