LAB // 01 • HEALTHCARE AI Active Investigation

Personalized Medical Recommendation System Using Explainable AI

Developing an interpretable machine learning system integrating model-agnostic Explainable AI (XAI) techniques—specifically SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations)—to formulate individualized clinical recommendations.

The project resolves the critical "black-box" dilemma in medical AI by translating complex non-linear feature interactions into human-comprehensible visual evidence, empowering medical practitioners to trust and corroborate algorithm decisions.

LAB // 02 • DEEP LEARNING Active Investigation

Emotion Detection from Facial Images

Engineering deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) pipelines trained on diverse facial expression datasets to classify primary human emotions in real time under unconstrained real-world environments.

The experimental design focuses on addressing challenging variance in facial angles, low-light conditions, partial facial occlusion, and demographic representation through advanced data augmentation and residual feature extraction.
